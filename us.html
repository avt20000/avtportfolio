Urban Symphony

Project Type: Class, Integrative Studio 2

Mediums: html/css/java, procreate, audio

Skills: web design, javascript, illustration, audio editing

Urban Symphony was developed from an open-ended prompt called "the voice", resulting in an interactive website experience indexing raw audio recorded around various neighborhoods of Manhattan. The aim was to show users that these neighborhoods, while being close in proximity, are actually quite auditorily distinct from one another. Each has certain sounds that identify them as that one specific place, which helps distinguish them from each other. As a goal, the website maps these auditory landscapes in an attempt to capture the “voice” of these neighborhoods.
