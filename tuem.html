Twitter Under Elon Musk

Project Type: Class, Typography 1

Mediums: Indesign, procreate

Skills: layout, printing, illustration

This book resulted from a wiki book project where we had to choose a wikipedia page and develop it into a physical object. I chose "Twitter under Elon Musk" because I was interested in collecting the sequence of events that occured under his takeover in one place. The format was an accordian-folded booklet that mimicked both the twitter layout and had the feeling of holding a mobile device. The accordian-folding was chosen to emulate the "infinity scroll" mechanism twitter uses. I then broke each segment down with caricature-style illustrations, to reference the large artistic community on twitter and to insert my own opinions around Elon Musk
