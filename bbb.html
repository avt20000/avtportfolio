Bite by Bite

Project Type: Class, Integrated Studio 2

Medium(s): indesign

Skills: layout, typography

Working in a team of four, my groupmates and I interviewed other students at our university to find out how they interact with restaurant culture in New York. We then transcribed and compiled these interviews into a book. My role was designing the layout, which I developed in Indesign using the transcribed texts and illustrations by group member Rachel Yan.
