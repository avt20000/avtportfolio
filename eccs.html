East Coast Culture Shock

Project Type: Class, Interaction 1

Mediums: html/css/java, procreate

Skills: web design, javascript, illustration

This wesbite was a result of a semester-long assignment to develop a "harmonic collection", with ten entries all focusing on a specific theme. My theme was "East Coast Culture Shock", which aimed to document my move from my hometown on the west coast to New York City. The website was designed to be highly interactive, which is shown below in the video documentation. I used a very illustrative style as it felt most effective to representing me and my story.
