Radical Risograph Posters

Project Type: Class, Radical Risograph

Mediums: indesign, hand illustration, photoshop, risograph

Skills: layout, typography, illustration, printing

This project was part of a class on Radical Risograph practices. I used a list of 30 wishes for the future the author Eduardo Galeano had laid out in his book Upside Down: A Primer for the Looking Glass World and paired a select few with my own illustrations. These were then printed in several color combinations on a risograph machine.
